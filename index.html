    Flood risk, explained

    A “1-in-100-year flood” isn’t once a century.

    It means a flood that size has a 1% chance of happening in any year. Drag the slider to see how far the water reaches around Aluva on the Periyar in each scenario, and what the odds look like over the 30 years you might live in a house.
